Biography

I am an ICT specialist with more than ten years of experience designing and scaling national digital governance systems, with a record of building real-time architectures and data-driven platforms at government level. I hold a BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (CGPA 3.78/4.00, Cum Laude).

Currently, I work in the ICT branch of the Ministry of Chittagong Hill Tracts Affairs, where I manage national platforms such as D-Nothi, GRS, GPMS and RMS, and led the technical roadmap of the Integrated Digital Service Delivery Platform. Before government service, I worked as a senior software engineer for Japanese global clients, and as an electrical engineer at the Bangladesh Rural Electrification Board.

I am now moving into biomedical engineering, artificial intelligence and data science, to build intelligent systems for health and the public sector using wearable sensing, large-scale data, predictive modelling and machine learning.

Research Interests

My base is electrical engineering, signal processing and numerical modelling, together with ten years of building large-scale software systems. I plan to apply these to graduate research in biomedical engineering, with a focus on wearable sensing and AI for health.
